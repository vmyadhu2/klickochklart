The approach

Start with one useful automation, not a giant system.

The first goal is to learn from real workflows: what takes time, what data is reliable, and where automation can safely remove repetitive work.

1. Pick a narrow task Choose one recurring Fortnox-related routine with a clear before and after.
2. Build the first version Create a small automation using scripts, APIs, files, or lightweight dashboards.
3. Productize what repeats Turn repeated patterns into a backend service and later a SaaS product.

Automation ideas

Useful places to begin.

These are practical starting points for businesses already using Fortnox and spending too much time on repeated admin.

INV

Invoice checks

Find unpaid invoices, missing references, overdue customers, or cases that need a reminder before they become noise.

REP

Recurring reports

Generate weekly summaries from Fortnox data and send them by email, spreadsheet, or a simple web view.

CSV

File cleanup

Transform CSV or Excel exports, validate fields, and prepare structured files for accounting or operations.

SYNC

Data sync support

Move selected data between Fortnox and other tools when manual copying is slow, fragile, or easy to forget.

About us

Fewer complicated steps. More things getting done.

Klick och Klart helps small businesses in Sweden save time by automating everyday work in Fortnox.

Many business owners spend hours on repetitive administration, such as handling invoices, updating customer information, preparing reports, and moving data between different systems. We create practical automation solutions that make these tasks faster, simpler, and less prone to errors.

Our services are designed for the way your business actually works. Whether you run a consultancy, shop, service company, or growing small business, we build solutions that fit your routines and connect smoothly with Fortnox.

The goal is simple: less manual administration, better control, and more time to focus on your customers and your business.

Klick och Klart means exactly that: fewer complicated steps and more things getting done.
